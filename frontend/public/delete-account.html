ExpTracker Data Privacy

Account & Data Deletion Request

At ExpTracker, you have full ownership and control of your personal information and financial records. Under Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), you can request complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Important: Deleting your ExpTracker account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your tracked accounts, transactions, transfers, categories, and budget logs will be permanently erased.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • User Profile: Your name, email address, password hash, and profile details.
  • Financial Accounts: Cash, Bank, and Mobile Wallet accounts.
  • Transaction History: All recorded expenses, incomes, transfers, and notes.
  • Budgets & Analytics: All historical budgets, categories, and monthly targets.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Immediate)

Log in to your ExpTracker app or web portal → Go to Settings → Profile → Request Account Deletion.

Option 2: Email Request (Web Deletion Request)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, submit a deletion request to our privacy team:

Send an email to support@shadiqur.bd from the email address registered with your ExpTracker account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Our team will process and purge your data within 7 business days and confirm via return email.
