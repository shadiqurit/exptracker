ExpTracker Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

Overview: ExpTracker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and web services collect, store, protect, and use your personal and financial information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ExpTracker, we collect the following types of information to provide personal finance tracking services:

  • Account & Profile Information: Your full name, email address, password hash (encrypted using Argon2id), and profile picture (if signing in via Google OAuth).
  • Financial Tracking Data: Information you voluntarily input, including financial accounts (Bank, Cash, Mobile Wallets like bKash/Nagad), transaction records (income, expense, transfers), category designations, dates, notes, and monthly budget targets.
  • Technical & Device Data: Minimal diagnostic data such as device operating system version, browser type, and API request logs to ensure system reliability and security.

2. What We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not collect or store your real bank account numbers, ATM PINs, credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank login credentials.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide and improve the ExpTracker service, specifically to:

  • Authenticate your identity and secure your financial records.
  • Calculate account balances, double-entry financial ledgers, and net wealth summaries.
  • Generate analytics, budget consumption alerts, and exportable financial reports (PDF, Excel, CSV).
  • Provide technical support and respond to your inquiries.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement enterprise-grade security standards to protect your sensitive financial information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the ExpTracker mobile app and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Data Isolation: User financial records are strictly isolated at the database level. No user can view or access another user's accounts, transactions, or budgets.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are protected using high-security Argon2id hashing algorithms.

5. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party services that comply with strict privacy policies:

  • Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0): Used solely to enable fast, secure single sign-on if you choose "Sign in with Google". Google receives no financial data from ExpTracker.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Hosted on secured cloud servers with strict firewall access rules and automated backups.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your personal and financial information:

  • Retention: Your data is retained as long as your account remains active to provide continuous historical reports.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated financial records. You can request account deletion at any time by visiting our Account Deletion Page or by emailing our privacy team. Upon deletion, all your personal information, accounts, transactions, and budgets are permanently purged from our primary database.

7. Children's Privacy

ExpTracker is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
